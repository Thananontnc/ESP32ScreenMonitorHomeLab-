Pringle Desk Panel

Mockup of the 2.8" CYD screen for the homelab, drawn at its real 320 × 240 pixels and scaled up. Tap the tabs and buttons on the screen. The numbers are examples.

Every size here is in real screen pixels. The tabs are 80 × 40 px and the buttons 44 px tall, because the resistive touch is not precise.

Homelab state

Same moods as the Mac menu bar app: Pringle runs when all is well (faster when CPU is high), looks worried on a problem, and sleeps when the homelab can't be reached.

UX rules for the panel

  • Readable from 2 mHome shows one mood and three numbers. Details live one tap away.
  • Status shows 3 waysPringle's pose, the colored pill and the board's RGB LED all agree, so you can see a problem without reading anything.
  • Big targetsNothing tappable under 40 px. The bottom tabs stay in the same place on every screen.
  • Safe actions"Pause ads" asks for a second tap, runs a 10-minute timer and always shows a Resume button.
  • Calm at nightThe light sensor (GPIO 34) dims the backlight. After 23:00 the screen sleeps until touched.
  • One data sourceThe panel reads the same /api/menubar JSON as the Mac app, so both always agree.
